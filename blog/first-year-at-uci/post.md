---
title: "Reflecting on my first year at UCI"
date: "12/2025"
excerpt: "More happened in one year than I expected — hackathons, real projects, new languages, and figuring out what kind of engineer I want to be."
---
<p>I came into UCI knowing how to code but not really knowing how to build. There's a difference.</p>

<p>The coursework gave me fundamentals I was missing — data structures, systems thinking, how computers actually work. But the biggest growth happened outside the classroom. I joined Commit the Change and started designing for a real client. I entered Webjam and won. I kept building side projects and they kept getting better.</p>

<p>The jump from high school programming to UCI-level engineering was real. The pace is faster, the expectations are higher, and nobody is going to remind you to start assignments early. I had to get organized fast.</p>

<p>The thing I'm most proud of from this year isn't any single project — it's that I figured out my process. I plan before I code. I design before I build. I think about who uses the thing, not just how it works. That shift happened gradually over the year and it shows up in everything I've built since.</p>

<p>Year two starts with the Army National Guard, CollIDE, Zotletics, and whatever else shows up. I'm ready for it.</p>
