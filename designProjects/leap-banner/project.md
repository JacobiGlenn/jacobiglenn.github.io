<p class="project-detail-lead"><strong>LEAP Banner</strong> came after the esports banners. LEAP Youth Alliance is a drug prevention coalition in Oregon. This one was a coloring banner for Red Ribbon Week: line art of a movie set, with the marquee reading “Life is a movie. Film drug free,” October 23–31, 2024. It went up at a local city-wide event so youth could color it while they learned about drug prevention. After that, it was hung.</p>
