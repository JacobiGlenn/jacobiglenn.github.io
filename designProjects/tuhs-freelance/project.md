<p class="project-detail-lead"><strong>Tualatin High School Freelance</strong> is the school work that sat outside the esports identity. Pack leader shirts, the Physics Extravaganza brochure, a LEAP Club kickoff poster, and summer tennis lessons.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Pack Leader</h2>
<p>Pack Leader is the logo for the pack leader work shirts, printed on the back. Pack leaders are juniors and seniors who mentor the sophomores. We came in a day early to show them around, and about once a month we went to their class to check in and hold a community meeting.</p>
<p>The paw started in the notebook, with the words sitting in the pads. That is the mark that got printed. I had also suggested a wolf. In 2025 the school put out a new wolf logo, and a lot of the design work followed it once that was out. The paw still won.</p>
