<p class="project-detail-lead"><strong>TuHS Broadcasting Logo</strong> is the mark for the Tualatin High School broadcast crew. The team recorded basketball and football and livestreamed the games on NFHS. The shirts said Broadcast Crew on the back, and this logo sat on the front.</p>
