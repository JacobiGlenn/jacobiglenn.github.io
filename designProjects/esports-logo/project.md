<p class="project-detail-lead"><strong>Esports Logo</strong> is the mark I made for Tualatin High School esports. I was one of the designers in the digital arts department, so when the school went through a branding change I ended up on a lot of it. I worked on this through most of junior year, then again around November and December of senior year, after the mascot changed.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">The first mark</h2>
<p>This is the mark from when esports started, before the senior-year mascot change. The wolf sits in a shield, which took its shape from the Warner Bros. shield. That version went on the wall outside the room.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Half wolf, half tech</h2>
<p>Once the school had a new wolf, I split the face: one half the mascot, the other half circuitry. I thought the esports head coach would like that. He wanted something simpler that would sit on a jersey and iron on cleanly, and he liked how the wolf-head shield read. So we dropped the extra parts and kept an outline of the wolf with techno eyes.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">What they use</h2>
<p>After some trial and error we set the words Tualatin Esports into the mane and kept the techno eyes. That is the main logo. They also use an alternate without the wording in the wolf, and a third mark that is just the eyes, usually on longer pieces like the backs of jerseys or YouTube banners.</p>
