<p class="project-detail-lead"><a href="https://www.instagram.com/tualatinboystennis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">@tualatinboystennis</a> is the Instagram I ran for Tualatin High School boys tennis in spring 2025, January-ish through June. I worked closely with the two head coaches to overhaul how they wanted their identity online to look and what the future branding of the sport would look like. </p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Before</h2>
<p>The feed before me was preset Canva posts where none of the posts had the same size or style. A match result, an announcement, and a senior night all looked like different accounts. It was hard to tell what was an upcoming game and what had already happened. I wanted to make this distinction clear and make sure to highlight the players instead of using generic graphics.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">New Style</h2>
<p>The coaches wanted a cleaner, more professional look that still matched the TUHS branding. They needed the match location and who we were playing, but the info needed to be readable quickly. Instead of a photo with a paragraph on it like they were doing before, we opted for using logos to convey most of the information. 

<br><br>
I gave the team a few directions to choose from, but they ended up deciding on a side bar with the school colors and logos. We had 3 or 4 iterations that had designs in the bar or more going on stylistically around the player, but the coaches liked the simplicity and readability of this design the most. 

<br><br>
After the first couple posts, the coaches asked for one small change to the red bar coloring so it more closely aligned with our team jerseys. 
</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">The Updated Feed</h2>
<p>A mix of GAMEDAY posts (to show UPCOMING games, graphic bar on the side) and HIGHLIGHT posts (for after games, in person photography). Senior night got it's own special post though, we added a photography post for the UPCOMING post and a video for the HIGHLIGHT post. Average likes increased from 24 to 31 on average. Engagement went up as well! The reach grew slightly but a lower reach is to be expected because our target audience is sub 800 people.</p>
