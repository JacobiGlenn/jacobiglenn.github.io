<p class="project-detail-lead"><strong>Boys Tennis</strong> is the Instagram I ran for Tualatin High School boys tennis in spring 2025, January through June. <a href="https://www.instagram.com/tualatinboystennis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">@tualatinboystennis</a>.</p>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Before</h2>
<p>The feed was preset Canva posts. Nothing shared a size. A match result, an announcement, and a senior night all looked like different accounts. A districts poster, a named spotlight, and a home match could sit next to each other and you still could not tell an event from a match that had not happened yet.</p>

  <img src="/designProjects/boys-tennis/before.jpg" alt="Earlier Tualatin boys tennis Instagram grid of Canva templates, with player spotlights, a districts poster, senior night, and a home match in different layouts" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Before. Preset templates, and no shared size.</figcaption>
</figure>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">The bar</h2>
<p>I brought the team a few directions. They wanted it cleaner. Where the match was, fast, instead of a photo with a paragraph on it. The player in the middle. The wolf huge.</p>
<p>The gameday post is a red bar and the player. GAMEDAY, the wolf, VS, the other school, and @HOME or the away name at the bottom. The red started as the school color. After two posts they asked for it darker, closer to the jersey than the school red.</p>

  <img src="/designProjects/boys-tennis/gameday.jpg" alt="Gameday post for Tualatin boys tennis versus Cartlin Gable at home, with a dark red bar, a large wolf logo, and the player centered on the court" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Cartlin Gable, April 21, at home. The bar after they asked for it darker.</figcaption>
</figure>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">The feed</h2>
<p>Home, Lake Oswego, and Lakeridge all use that bar. Senior night and the score sit in the same feed. Average likes went from 24 to 31.</p>
