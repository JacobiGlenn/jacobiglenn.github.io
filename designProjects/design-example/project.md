<p class="project-detail-reader-note">Other design entries on this site follow the same beats: intro, visuals with captions, how it was made, and a closing note. This one keeps the subject tiny on purpose.</p>
<p class="project-detail-lead">The whole assignment: draw a friendly smiley as a <strong>circle</strong> with <strong>: )</strong> inside (colon, space, paren) on <strong>one horizontal line</strong>, not stacked upright.</p>
<div class="smiley-face-demo" aria-hidden="true"><div class="smiley-circle" title=": )"><span>: )</span></div></div>
<p>Padding between the smiley and the inside edge of the circle mattered as much as the characters. Too little and the face felt crowded. Too much and it stopped feeling warm or friendly. The sweet spot was enough air so nothing touched the ring, but still read as one tight, happy mark.</p>
<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Early passes</h2>
<p>First tries were too oval, or the ring sat too heavy. Grayscale comps focused on a single stroke weight for the circle and monospace-adjacent punctuation so <code>: )</code> didn&rsquo;t fight the curve.</p>
<figure class="project-detail-fig"><div class="ph-img" style="padding:0;"><div class="shape-study" aria-hidden="true"><div class="shape-oval"></div><div class="shape-tri"></div><div class="shape-sq"></div></div></div><figcaption>Oval, then triangle, then square. Finding a genuinely perfect circle was the hardest part, and it took way longer than drawing these three. The compass tool started as a mystery and turned into something I leaned on every session.</figcaption></figure>
<h2 class="project-detail-h2">How the face settled</h2>
<p>Iteration was mostly optical: nudging the pair so the smile lines up with the implied baseline of the circle, checking at small sizes, and making sure the colon doesn&rsquo;t read like eyes stacked vertically when someone scans quickly.</p>
<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Timeline</h2>
<ul class="project-detail-timeline">
<li><span class="tl-date">Week 1 &mdash; Circle</span>Perfecting a perfect circle: compass tool, pixel snapping, and killing the slight egg shape that kept creeping in.</li>
<li><span class="tl-date">Week 2 &mdash; : )</span>Locking <code>: )</code> on one line with deliberate space, never upright or cramped, so it still reads as a smile from arm&rsquo;s length.</li>
<li><span class="tl-date">Week 3 &mdash; Balance</span>Centering the pair inside the ring so the negative space feels even; adjusting letter-spacing instead of stretching glyphs.</li>
<li><span class="tl-date">Week 4 &mdash; Finish</span>Final stroke weight, export at 1x and 2x, and a tiny one-line spec so the mark reproduces the same in slides and on the site.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="project-detail-h2">Takeaway</h2>
<p>building the perfect smiley face was hard and left me figuring out how to use a compass tool in ways i never knew possible!</p>
